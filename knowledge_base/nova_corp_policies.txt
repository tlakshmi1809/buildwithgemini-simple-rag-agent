# NovaSmart Corporate Policies & FAQ

## Working Hours & Remote Work
NovaSmart operates on a flexible hybrid work model. Core working hours are 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM local time. Employees are eligible for up to 3 days per week remote work with manager approval.

## Travel Expense Reimbursement
- Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of travel.
- Daily meal allowance is $75 per day.
- Flights under 6 hours must be booked in Economy or Economy Plus.

## Security & AI Governance Policy
- All AI agents deployed into production must be registered in the official Agent Registry catalog.
- Shared service accounts are prohibited; every production agent must use its own distinct Agent Identity.
- Least-privilege IAM roles must be enforced on all agent service accounts.
